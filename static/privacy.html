Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Outrovo is an AI people-search service. This policy explains, in plain language, what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Data about people you search for

When you run a search, we gather publicly available professional information about individuals from public sources (Wikipedia, Wikidata, GitHub, Bluesky, Stack Overflow, OpenAlex, Hacker News, DEV.to, Mastodon, Product Hunt, YouTube, and personal websites). We show this information to you and keep a copy in our index so future searches are faster and better.

We do not buy data, scrape private or login-walled content, or infer sensitive attributes (health, political views, ethnicity, sexuality). The information we surface is what people have chosen to publish about themselves professionally.

If you are listed in our index and want your profile corrected or removed, email privacy@outrovo.com and we will act within 30 days (usually much faster). This is your right under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws.

2. Data about you (our customer)

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password (we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Your activity: searches you run, outreach messages you send, and your thumbs up/down feedback. This data is yours alone — it is scoped to your account and not shared with other users.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

3. Email open tracking

Outreach emails you send through Outrovo contain a tiny invisible image (a 1×1 pixel) hosted on our servers. If the recipient's email client loads images, we record that the message was opened, when it was first opened, and how many times. We show this only to you, in your private outreach log.

Many email clients (including Apple Mail and most corporate gateways) block or preload images, so "not opened" does not prove the message was unread, and "opened" does not prove a human read it. Use this signal as a hint, not a fact.

You are the sender of these emails. You are responsible for complying with email laws that apply to you and your recipients (CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR/PECR in the EU/UK, CASL in Canada) — including honoring opt-outs and identifying yourself accurately.

4. What we never do

  • Sell your data or the data of people in our index.
  • Share your searches, outreach, or feedback with other customers.
  • Auto-send follow-ups — every message is reviewed and sent by you.
  • Use your data to train AI models for third parties.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

For EU/UK individuals found in searches, our lawful basis is legitimate interest: indexing publicly published professional information to help people connect for business purposes. You may object at any time via the removal address above, and we will honor it.

6. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. Search results are cached for a limited time and refreshed from live sources. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at privacy@outrovo.com.

7. Security

Passwords are PBKDF2-hashed. Sessions use random bearer tokens that expire after 30 days. Traffic is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever suffer a breach affecting your data, we will notify you promptly.

8. Contact

Questions, corrections, removal or deletion requests: privacy@outrovo.com.
