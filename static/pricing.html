Questions

What counts as a search?

One natural-language query that runs live across our data sources and returns AI-scored results. Re-opening a previous search from your history is free. Quotas reset every day at midnight UTC.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources only: Wikipedia, Wikidata, GitHub, Bluesky, OpenAlex, Hacker News, DEV.to, Mastodon, Product Hunt, YouTube, Hugging Face, npm, arXiv, PubMed, DBLP, Crossref, ORCID, MusicBrainz, Mixcloud, Dailymotion, Open Library, Codeberg, Lobsters, 180+ Stack Exchange communities, and personal websites. We never scrape private or login-walled content.

How does email outreach work?

Outrovo finds publicly published email addresses, verifies the domain can receive mail, drafts a personalized message, and sends it through your connected email account. An invisible 1×1 pixel tells you when it's opened. Follow-ups are drafted for your review but never sent automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in one click and your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then returns to Free. Your lists and history are preserved.

Is my data private?

Your searches, lists, and outreach are scoped to your account and never shared with other users. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture, including how people found in searches can request removal.
